The 40-year story

Four decades on the western Green Bay shoreline.

One waterfront. Two generations. Five service lines. The longest-tenured family-owned full-service marina on this stretch of the bay.

From Shipyard Marine to Marine 365.

The dealership has been operating at 780 Longtail Beach Road in Suamico since 1985. For its first three and a half decades the firm operated as Shipyard Marine. In approximately 2020 the rebrand to Marine 365 marked a generational transition while the same family ownership, the same Longtail Beach Road waterfront, and the same five-line operation carried through unchanged.

Across forty years a Suamico family has built something unusual on Green Bay: a single-location marina that does new sales, pre-owned sales, slip and dry-stack storage, full mechanical service, and parts under one waterfront roof. Few dealers in the regional comparison run all five at depth. None of the smaller specialty operators do.

That depth is what the Top 100 Boat Dealer judges from Boating Industry magazine recognize when they score the firm against roughly 7,000 marine dealers across North America. It is what the Chaparral and Robalo factory teams confirm when they renew the authorized-dealer franchises. It is what a Suamico boater experiences when they take a Chaparral home in March, store it on the water in May, run it through the season, drop it for winterization in October, and pick up parts in January.

Five lines, one waterfront

Everything a Wisconsin boater needs, on the same Suamico dock.

Sales. Slip and dry-stack storage. Service across inboards, outboards, and sterndrives. Winterization for the Wisconsin freeze. Parts when you need them.

01

New & Pre-Owned Boat Sales

Authorized Chaparral and Robalo new-boat sales plus a continuously rotating pre-owned inventory of trade-ins, consignments, and brokered listings. Every pre-owned hull inspected by Marine 365 service technicians before listing. Wisconsin DMV title-transfer and US Coast Guard documentation handled per state and federal rules.

02

Slip & Dry-Stack Storage

Slip storage and dry-stack storage at the Longtail Beach Road marina on the western Green Bay shoreline. Forklift launch and retrieval included on dry-stack. Slip dimensions, dry-stack length and weight limits, and seasonal availability firm-confirmed at the marina office.

03

Mechanical Service

Inboards, outboards, and sterndrives across the major engine families. Routine maintenance, water-pump and lower-unit service, ignition and electrical diagnostics, fuel-system service, and warranty work on covered hulls. Service team works the same factory-trained schedule that the Top 100 judges score against.

04

Winterization & Spring Commissioning

Wisconsin winter brings ice on Green Bay typically December through March. Full winterization includes engine fogging, water-system draining, antifreeze, fuel stabilizer, oil and lower-unit service, battery handling, and shrink-wrapping. Spring commissioning covers de-winterization, fluids, hull inspection, electronics check, and a launch-readiness sea trial when weather permits.

05

Fiberglass, Gelcoat & Parts

Fiberglass and gelcoat repair from cosmetic refinishing through structural transom and stringer work, with color matching for both Chaparral and Robalo factory finishes. The parts counter stocks OEM Chaparral and Robalo plus a rotating inventory of common service parts, electronics, safety equipment, and accessories.

06

Shipping & Delivery

Marine 365 coordinates over-the-road boat shipping for sold units across Wisconsin and the Upper Midwest. Trailer transport, hauler scheduling, and delivery insurance handled per the firm’s standard sale and delivery agreement. Multi-state coverage where the buyer is located.

The western Green Bay shoreline

780 Longtail Beach Road, Suamico.

Twelve miles north of downtown Green Bay. The protected end of the western shoreline. Slip water, dry stack, showroom, and service shop on one parcel.

Why this stretch of the bay.

The western Green Bay shoreline runs from the Fox River mouth north through Howard, Suamico, and into Oconto County. Suamico sits at the protected mid-point: enough open water for serious bay boating, sheltered enough for safe slip and dry-stack storage through the typical Wisconsin season.

Marine 365’s Longtail Beach Road parcel sits directly on that shoreline. The property combines a working marina (slip plus dry-stack) with a full showroom for new and pre-owned Chaparral and Robalo inventory and a service shop for the mechanical, fiberglass, winterization, and spring-commissioning work the season demands.

Drive time: 18 minutes from downtown Green Bay; 28 minutes from De Pere; 22 minutes from Howard; 35 minutes from Oconto; 45 minutes from Marinette; about an hour from Appleton via the Fox Valley feeder route.
